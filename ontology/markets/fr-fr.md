# French (France) (fr-fr)

- **Native Name**: Français, France
- **Direction**: ltr

## Market Snapshot

Audience: Independent artists, bedroom producers, singer-songwriters, beatmakers, content creators (YouTube/TikTok/Twitch), music educators, and semi-professional bands. France ranks among the top 10 global music markets. The home studio movement is strong, driven by affordable hardware and cloud-native tools. A notable creative class spans genres — variété française, rap/hip-hop, electronic, and hybrid contemporary pop.
Primary jobs-to-be-done: Compose and produce music independently, collaborate across cities, master and export release-ready tracks, distribute via streaming platforms.
Key sensitivities:

Authorship & droits d'auteur are deeply embedded in French legal and cultural identity. SACEM membership and rights registration are the norm for active creators. Any AI-assisted creation raises immediate questions about who holds the copyright (French law requires a human author). This is not abstract anxiety — it is legally active territory as of 2025–2026, with a Senate bill proposing presumption of AI exploitation of protected works.
AI skepticism among established creators: 82% of producers globally cite artistic integrity as a reason to avoid full AI adoption. In France, this is amplified by a strong cultural tradition of auteur craft. Framing AI as a tool, not a co-creator, is not optional — it is a legal and reputational requirement.
EU AI Act compliance (Aug 2025 onwards): Generative AI providers must publish training data summaries. French creators are aware of this. Transparency messaging will be expected, not exceptional.
SACEM/ADAMI/SPEDIDAM ecosystem: French creators operate within a collective rights management culture. Any AI feature that could blur ownership lines must be handled with explicit user-agency cues.

Sérieuse sans être froide — competent, not cold; credible without corporate distance
Directe — short, clear sentences; action-forward

Vous for: billing, legal notices, formal enterprise/education contexts, and error messages in high-stakes flows.

Short, active voice, imperative for actions.

Avoid:

Hype language: "révolutionnaire", "magique", "sans effort", "parfait"
Anglicisms where French equivalents exist and feel natural

Over-explaining — French readers respond to confidence and brevity

## Culture & Pragmatics

Value signals in France for music tools:
"Qualité professionnelle", "contrôle total", "flux de travail", "exportation propre", "tes droits", "ta musique".
AI framing — critical for this market:

Position IA as assistant, copilote, outil de suggestion — never créateur or compositeur.
Explicitly reinforce user control and authorship: "L'IA propose. Tu décides."
Given active French legislative debate on AI and droits voisins (Senate bill Dec 2025), transparency is a trust signal, not a legal formality. Use it proactively.
Never imply that AI-generated output is publication-ready without human validation — this has legal implications under French droit d'auteur.

Community angle: Collaboration and sharing are normal, but the individual creator's signature artistique is paramount. Avoid language that dissolves individual identity into collective output.

## Grammar & Mechanics

French (France) standard — not Canadian French, not Belgian.
Imperative for UI actions: "Crée", "Enregistre", "Glisse", "Ajuste", "Exporte"
Avoid gerund constructions borrowed from English UI patterns:

❌ "Nous exportons ta piste" → ✅ "Exportation en cours…"


Accents and punctuation correct with no exceptions (é, è, ê, ç, œ, etc.)
Proper French quotation marks: « » with non-breaking spaces inside: « comme ça »
No anglicised verb constructions: ❌ "te connecter" (as a calque for "log in") → ✅ "se connecter" or "ouvrir une session"
Inclusive writing: use inclusive forms where appropriate for community/plural contexts — "les créateur·rice·s" — but keep UI microcopy to the simpler gender-neutral or masculine default for brevity.

## UX / Microcopy Rules

Buttons: verb + object, 2–3 words

"Créer un projet", "Enregistrer une piste", "Exporter le mix", "Ajouter une piste"

System status:

Progress: "Préparation…" / "Envoi en cours…" / "Exportation…"
Success: "C'est prêt. Tu peux le télécharger."
Error: explain + action + reassurance

"Impossible d'envoyer le fichier. Vérifie ta connexion et réessaie."



Empty states: invite + hint

"Aucune piste pour l'instant. Ajoute-en une ou importe de l'audio pour commencer."

Tooltips: one sentence, outcome-driven, ≤ 120 characters

"Quantise pour caler le rythme sur la grille sans perdre le naturel du jeu."

AI transparency cue (mandatory at every AI touchpoint):

"L'IA suggère. Tu choisis."
"C'est une proposition — modifie, garde ou supprime."

## Terminology Rules

AI wording:

Use: IA, assistant, suggestions, variantes, copilote créatif
Avoid: "l'IA compose pour toi", "musique automatique sans effort" (sounds like replacement / undermines droits d'auteur)

Do not translate plan names: Creator Starter, Creator Pro, Creator Max, Studio remain in English per ontology policy.
Regulatory note: Avoid any phrasing that implies AI-generated content is automatically the user's property without their creative input — this is active legal territory in France (SACEM opt-out, AI Act transparency obligations, pending Senate legislation).
